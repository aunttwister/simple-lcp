Poll for latest data

This reads the public LMArena leaderboards and shows you what has changed since the numbers you last kept.

  • Only models a provider here actually serves are read. Every other name on the board is ignored — nothing is invented.
  • A rating is shown as Elo (text boards, around 1500) or as a net improvement (agent boards, small signed values), plus a percentile. The two are different measurements and are never ranked against each other.
  • Polling saves nothing. Tick the rows you want and press Update to publish them; Clear throws the polled results away.
  • Updating writes the leaderboard only. The capability matrix the router sorts by is not touched.
