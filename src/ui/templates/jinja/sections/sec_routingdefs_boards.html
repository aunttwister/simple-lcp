Poll

What this reads. LMArena's own published boards — the same files the arena website renders: a text arena where models are voted on head to head and published as an Elo rating, and agent arenas where a model is run on real tasks and published as a net improvement (tasks done better minus tasks done worse, as a signed score). Both carry a rank out of the models that board scored.

What it will NOT do. It cannot poll your own taxonomies. A custom: board is a rating you entered — there is no upstream to re-read, so a poll has nothing to fetch for it and never touches it. Custom boards change only when you edit or import them.

What happens to your numbers. Polling saves nothing: it stages a side-by-side of what each board says now against what you kept, with both dates. Tick the rows you want and press Update to publish them; Clear throws the whole staged set away and changes nothing.

What is ignored. Every arena name that does not resolve to a model a provider here serves — nothing is guessed at or invented.

Arena boards only. A poll reads the LMArena boards and nothing else. Your custom: boards are not in it, are never staged here and can never be written by it — they change only through Manage taxonomy.
